About

I am Xinyang Geng, currently pursuing my Ph.D. at the School of Computer Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I received my B.S. degree from the School of Cyber Science and Technology, Shandong University, ranking in the top 15% of my undergraduate cohort.

My research lies at the intersection of cryptography and AI, with a particular focus on homomorphic encryption, post-quantum cryptography, and AI security.
