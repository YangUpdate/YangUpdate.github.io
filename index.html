About 关于

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I received my B.E. degree in Cyberspace Security from Shandong University in 2025, where I ranked in the top 15% and was awarded the Outstanding Graduate honor. My research interests lie at the intersection of cryptography and artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on homomorphic encryption, post-quantum cryptography, and AI security.

我是上海交通大学计算机学院的博士一年级研究生。2025年本科毕业于山东大学网络空间安全学院，专业排名前15%，获校级优秀毕业生荣誉。研究方向聚焦于密码学与人工智能的交叉领域，特别关注同态加密、后量子密码和AI安全。
